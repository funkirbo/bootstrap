home

hey hello hi! i am funkirbo! i like to write about the things i do!

and when i say “things i do”, i really mean what i play; video games fill the vast majority of my free time.

this is by no means a comprehensive list of everything i’ve ever finished; writing takes time, and i only have so much of that. my goal is to slowly chip away at writing reviews for everything, but that’s sort of like sisyphus pushing the boulder, right? it’s a goal i know i’m never going to finish, because every game i finish pushes the posts back even further.

about me

i am currently a sophomore computer science major at a university i will not disclose. outside of university, i really don’t do all that much, to be honest. i play a lot of video games, listen to music, and hang out with my friends. at the end of the day, i’m a pretty boring guy.

genre-wise, i’m a big fan of mystery stuff. ace attorney is my favorite game franchise, and the two knives out movies i’ve seen have been excellent as well. i’ll enjoy pretty much anything you put in front of me, though, as long as it’s quality; i’m not all that picky. …that sounds kind of corny, but it’s true!

content

the reviews on this site are called “reviews” only because there’s really not a better way to describe them. i don’t focus on reaching a certain word length or anything, i just kind of talk until i’ve put down the thoughts i want to. that means that a good chunk of the reviews are pretty short, and even the longest ones aren’t much longer than a standard game review. that lack of length is a combination of my lack of writing skill, and the lack of interesting and unique thoughts i have about whatever i’m writing about. i'm not going to waste either of our time rehashing the thoughts of people smarter than i.

all of the reviews are also cross-posted to backloggd as well as infinite backlog. i try to stay active on both. if you’d like to see my full collection, which is basically a centralized log of everything i own, alongside anything i’ve finished, that is located on my spreadsheet!

if you notice any issues on the site (typos/formatting errors), on the spreadsheet, or in my reviews, please let me know! my socials are just to the side (or above if you're on a smaller device), and my discord is @funkirbo!
